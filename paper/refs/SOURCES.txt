Reference PDFs for the paper (local archive so they are not lost).
All items are open-access (arXiv / HAL / CEUR-WS, CC-BY or equivalent).

2023-clerice-glaise-pseudo-chrysostoms.pdf
  Clerice, Glaise. "Twenty-One* Pseudo-Chrysostoms and more: authorship
  verification in the patristic world." CHR 2023, CEUR-WS. hal-04211176v2.
  The direct predecessor this paper reproduces and extends: Siamese network +
  signal-to-noise-ratio distance over MFW/affix/POS-trigram features, AUC-ROC
  84.5%, verdicts against Voicu's 21 pseudo-Chrysostoms (Table 5: C/HP/LP/R/U).

severian-genuine-works-cpg.pdf, severian-genuine-works-cpg-v2.pdf
  Compilations of Severian of Gabala's genuine works with CPG numbers
  (CPG 4185-4295), used to check the ground-truth attributions (e.g. PC9 = CPG 4215
  "In illud: Pater, transeat"; Severian's Genesis = CPG 4194, distinct from PC1's
  CPG 4410). Provenance: CPG II / Roger Pearse's CPG-based compilation.
